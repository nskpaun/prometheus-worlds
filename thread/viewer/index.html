Stitch worlds

New world

Pick 20 to 60 overlapping photos of one room or place. Walk around while shooting; do not stand in one spot. Takes a few minutes.
Or pick a single photo, indoors or out: it becomes a scene to look into from about where it was taken, in under a minute.

Describe the place in a sentence or two: what kind of room it is and the things that matter in it, with where they stand if you care. The rest is filled in to suit: more furniture and small things, the walls, windows and doors, materials and light. Say the time of day if it matters. A place outdoors (a garden, a street) is built in an older, simpler way, where the things you name become painted solid shapes and can move.

Describe the place. It is planned, sculpted and given materials as a world from a prompt is, then put together in Blender: the world model on this Mac builds the furniture shape by shape through the Blender MCP, looks at each piece and mends it, and Blender bakes the light. The result is solid geometry with soft, true light and no splat. Needs Blender installed. A place outdoors is always built the quick way.
